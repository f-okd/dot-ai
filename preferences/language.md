# Language and anti-slop rules

Slop-resistant communication preferences, adapted from [poteto's unslop skill](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/main/pstack/skills/unslop/SKILL.md).

Rules for all communication and written content.

## 1. Content and attributions
- **Superficial -ing phrases:** Cut dangling participial tails like "highlighting...", "ensuring...", "reflecting...", "showcasing...", "fostering...". Delete them or state the concrete mechanism.
- **Vague attributions:** Avoid "experts believe", "industry reports suggest", "some critics argue". Name the source or delete the attribution.
- **Excessive hedging:** Do not hedge every statement with "might possibly", "could potentially", "it seems that". Be direct.

## 2. Vocabulary and phrasing
- **Banned AI words:** *additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape* (abstract), *pivotal, showcase, tapestry* (abstract), *testament, underscore, vibrant*.
- **Fancy ways to say "is" or "has":** Avoid "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "features". Use "is" or "has".
- **"Not just X, but Y":** State the point directly instead of using contrast framing.
- **Rule of three:** Do not force ideas into artificial groups of three. Use the natural count.
- **Synonym cycling:** Do not cycle through synonyms to avoid repeating a word. Pick the precise technical term and reuse it consistently.
- **False ranges:** Avoid "from X to Y" when X and Y are not on a meaningful spectrum. List items directly.

## 3. Style and formatting
- **Em dash overuse:** Avoid em dashes entirely. Use periods or commas only (no en dashes, no parentheses, no hyphen-as-dash substitutes).
- **Colon overuse:** Colons are fine before a list or code block, but never as mid-sentence connectors.
- **Boldface overuse:** Do not bold every acronym, proper noun, or technical term.
- **Inline-header lists:** Avoid bold labels that repeat the following text (e.g. "**Performance:** Performance improved..."). Convert to plain prose.
- **Sentence case headings:** Use sentence case for all headings.
- **No decorative emojis:** Remove emojis from headings, bullets, and text.
- **Straight quotes:** Use straight quotes (' and ") instead of curly quotes.

## 4. Communication artifacts and filler
- **No sycophancy:** Do not open with flattery ("Great question!", "You're absolutely right!", "Certainly!", "Of course!").
- **No chatbot sign-offs:** Cut "I hope this helps!", "Let me know if you have questions!", "Happy coding!".
- **Cut filler:**
  - "In order to" -> "To"
  - "Due to the fact that" -> "Because"
  - Delete "It is important to note that" and "Keep in mind that" entirely.
